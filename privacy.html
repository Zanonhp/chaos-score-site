Privacy policy - Chaos Score uploader

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The Chaos Score uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chaos Score YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's basic information to confirm it is the right channel.

What it stores

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. The only credential it keeps is the owner's own access token, stored privately by the owner.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
